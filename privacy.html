繁體中文

一句話

LiveLED 沒有帳號、沒有屬於我們的伺服器。你輸入的跑馬燈文字、顏色、速度與預設檔全部留在你的裝置上，我們看不到，也無從取得。

App 會連線的情況只有三種：向 Google 取得廣告、向 Google Play 確認你是否已購買、以及回報崩潰與匿名使用統計。這三件事都不包含你輸入的任何文字內容。

我們不做什麼

  • 不要求註冊或登入，沒有任何帳號系統。
  • 不讀取你的通訊錄、相簿、位置或簡訊。
  • 不會把你輸入的跑馬燈文字傳送給任何人，廣告商也一樣。
  • 不會錄製聲音（麥克風的使用方式請見下方〈麥克風權限〉）。
  • 不販賣任何個人資料。

廣告

免費版在「設定頁」提供「補充時數」按鈕，你主動點擊後才會播放一支獎勵式廣告，看完可換取播放時長。廣告由 Google AdMob 提供。

播放跑馬燈的畫面完全沒有廣告——沒有橫幅、沒有插頁、沒有任何遮擋。這是本 App 的核心設計，不會改變。

為了送出廣告，Google 可能會取得你裝置的廣告識別碼（Advertising ID）、大致所在地區、裝置型號與語言等資訊，並依此決定顯示哪一則廣告。這部分適用 Google 的隱私權政策。我們不經手廣告內容，也看不到廣告向你顯示了什麼。

我們不會把 App 內的任何內容交給 Google——廣告不知道你打了什麼字、支持哪個藝人。

你可以在 Android 系統設定的「隱私權 › 廣告」中重設或刪除廣告 ID，也可以關閉個人化廣告。

購買永久版之後，充能按鈕會消失，App 不再請求任何廣告。

歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的使用者

如果你所在地區適用 GDPR 或同等規範，App 會在首次啟動廣告功能前顯示同意聲明視窗，讓你選擇是否接受個人化廣告。你可以隨時在設定頁的「重新設定廣告偏好」中更改這個選擇。

你就 Google 所處理的資料享有存取、更正、刪除與反對處理等權利，行使方式請參閱 Google 的隱私權政策。就我們自行處理的部分（見〈崩潰回報與使用統計〉），你可以來信 support@kyappstudio.com 要求刪除。

麥克風權限（選用，預設關閉）

App 有一個「隨音量律動」特效，會讓跑馬燈跟著現場音量閃動。這個特效預設為關閉，只有在你主動開啟時才會請求麥克風權限。

啟用後，App 的處理方式是：

  • 只讀取聲音的音量大小（振幅），不辨識內容、不轉成文字。
  • 不錄製、不儲存、不寫入任何檔案。 音訊資料在裝置記憶體中即時計算後立刻丟棄。
  • 不上傳到任何地方，包括我們與 Google。

不授權完全不影響其他功能，只是這個特效無法使用。你可以隨時在系統設定中收回權限。

購買與付款

永久版由 Google Play 處理。我們不會接觸你的付款資訊——App 只會從 Google Play 收到「這個 Google 帳號是否已購買」這一項結果。

購買紀錄保存在你的 Google 帳號中，因此換手機或重新安裝後可透過「恢復購買」還原。

崩潰回報

App 使用 Google Firebase Crashlytics，而且只用這一項：

服務收集內容用途
Crashlytics崩潰堆疊、Android 版本、機型修正錯誤，尤其是播放中黑畫面或崩潰

沒有使用行為分析。 我們不知道你開過幾次、用了哪些功能、播了多久。

這些資料不包含你輸入的跑馬燈文字，也不包含任何能直接識別你身分的資訊（沒有姓名、email、電話）。適用 Google 的隱私權政策。

你的資料存在哪裡

跑馬燈文字、顏色、速度、預設檔、剩餘應援時數與購買狀態都存在 App 自己的本機儲存空間，只有這個 App 讀得到。移除 App 就會一併刪除。

其中應援時數與購買狀態經過加密儲存，並已設定為排除在 Android 系統自動備份之外，不會被備份到你的 Google 雲端硬碟。

網路權限

網路權限用於：載入廣告、向 Google Play 查詢購買狀態、回報崩潰與統計、下載設定值。

跑馬燈的顯示、點陣渲染、特效、預設檔存取全部在裝置上完成，進場後開飛航模式仍可正常播放。

分享碼

「分享碼」功能會把你的設定（文字、顏色、速度、特效）編碼成一段文字或 QR 碼。這個編碼完全在你的裝置上產生，不經過任何伺服器。 要把它給誰、透過什麼方式傳，完全由你決定，我們無從得知。

兒童

本 App 不是為兒童設計的，主要對象是參加演唱會、體育賽事等現場活動的一般使用者。我們自己不蒐集任何人的個人資料。廣告部分由 Google 提供（見上方〈廣告〉），若使用者為兒童，建議由家長透過 Android 的家長監護功能管理。

政策變更

政策若有修改，會更新本頁上方的版本與生效日期。若變更涉及新增資料收集，會在 App 內主動告知。

English

In short

LiveLED has no accounts and no server of ours. The marquee text, colours, speed, and presets you enter stay on your device; we cannot see or obtain them.

The app connects to the network for exactly three purposes: requesting ads from Google, checking with Google Play whether you have purchased, and reporting crashes and anonymous usage statistics. None of these include any text you type.

What we do not do

  • No sign-up or login; there is no account system.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, or messages.
  • Your marquee text is never sent to anyone, advertisers included.
  • No audio is ever recorded (see Microphone permission below for how the microphone is used).
  • No personal data is sold.

Advertising

The free version offers a "Recharge" button on the Settings screen. Only when you tap it does a rewarded ad play, and watching it grants playback time. Ads are served by Google AdMob.

The marquee playback screen contains no advertising at all — no banners, no interstitials, no obstruction of any kind. This is a core design commitment and will not change.

To serve ads, Google may obtain your device's Advertising ID, approximate region, device model, and language, and use these to decide which ad to show. Google's Privacy Policy applies to that processing. We neither handle nor see the ad content shown to you.

We never hand any in-app content to Google — the ads do not know what you typed or which artist you support.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, and turn off personalised ads, under Privacy › Ads in Android settings.

After the lifetime purchase, the recharge button disappears and the app requests no ads at all.

Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

If GDPR or an equivalent regime applies to you, the app shows a consent dialog before enabling any advertising functionality, letting you choose whether to accept personalised ads. You can change that choice at any time via "Reset ad preferences" on the Settings screen.

Regarding data processed by Google, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, and objection; see Google's Privacy Policy for how to exercise them. For the parts we process ourselves (see Crash reporting and usage statistics), email support@kyappstudio.com to request deletion.

Microphone permission (optional, off by default)

The app has a "react to music" effect that makes the marquee pulse with ambient volume. This effect is off by default; the microphone permission is requested only when you turn it on yourself.

Once enabled:

  • Only the loudness (amplitude) of the sound is read. No content recognition, no speech-to-text.
  • Nothing is recorded, stored, or written to any file. Audio data is computed in memory in real time and immediately discarded.
  • Nothing is uploaded anywhere, including to us and to Google.

Declining changes nothing else; only this one effect becomes unavailable. You can revoke the permission at any time in system settings.

Purchases and payment

The lifetime purchase is handled by Google Play. We never touch your payment information — the app receives only one thing from Google Play: whether this Google account has purchased.

The purchase record lives in your Google account, so it can be restored via "Restore purchase" after changing phones or reinstalling.

Crash reporting

The app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics, and nothing else from Firebase:

ServiceWhat it collectsPurpose
CrashlyticsCrash stack traces, Android version, device modelFixing bugs, especially black screens or crashes during playback

No usage analytics. We do not know how often you open the app, which features you use, or how long you play.

None of this includes the marquee text you type, and none of it directly identifies you (no name, email, or phone number). Google's Privacy Policy applies.

Where your data lives

Marquee text, colours, speed, presets, remaining cheer time, and purchase state are stored in the app's own local storage, readable only by this app. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Cheer time and purchase state are stored encrypted and are excluded from Android's Auto Backup, so they are not backed up to your Google Drive.

Internet permission

The internet permission is used to: load ads, query purchase state from Google Play, report crashes and statistics, and download configuration values.

Marquee display, dot-matrix rendering, effects, and preset access all happen on your device — playback works normally in airplane mode once you are inside the venue.

Share codes

The "share code" feature encodes your settings (text, colours, speed, effect) into a short string or QR code. This encoding is produced entirely on your device and passes through no server. Whom you give it to, and how, is entirely your choice and unknown to us.

Children

This app is not designed for children; it is aimed at general users attending concerts, sporting events, and similar live events. We collect no personal data from anyone. Advertising is provided by Google (see Advertising above); where the user is a child, we recommend parents manage this through Android's parental controls.

Changes to this policy

Any revision updates the version and effective date at the top of this page. If a change involves collecting new data, we will say so in the app.
